Introduction to Emerging Technologies

Emerging technologies are innovative solutions that are currently being developed or implemented to address existing challenges and create new opportunities. These technologies often have the potential to significantly impact various industries and aspects of society.

Emerging technologies are typically new technologies discovered through united efforts and dedication to the craft.

Some examples of Emerging Technologies include

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Nanotechnology
  • Virtual Reality
  • Robotics
  • Quantum Computing
  • Biotechnology

These technologies are constantly evolving and have the potential to transform the way we live, work, and interact with the world around us.

Ajayi et. al., 2025

Emerging technologies are characterized by radical novelty (in application even if not in origins) relatively fast growth, coherence, prominent impact, and uncertainty and ambiguity.

Rotolo et. al., 2015

Emerging Technologies

Emerging technologies are often perceived as capable of changing the status quo.

Examples of Emerging Technologies: Advanced Robotics

Advanced Robotics utilizes robotic systems that can follow complex commands and reacting to situations intelligently This type of technology combines Robotics and Artificial Intelligence to create useful robots that can do impressive things.

Advanced Robotics, similar to most kinds of emerging technologies, continues to grow and improve upon its predecessors. Further adding to their complexity and their uses. Which can be significantly beneficial if used properly. Some products of Advanced Robotics include humanoid robots, advanced robotic arms and legs, exoskeletons and many more. (Robotnik, 2022)

However there are caveats, due to the possibilities of Advanced Robotics, other people could perhaps take advantage of their uses to create something undesireable. Which leads to people having mixed feelings about their existence. But nonetheless, Advanced Robotics still- is a form of new technology that undeniably has so much potential.

Examples of Emerging Technologies: Genetic Engineering

Genetic Engineering involves the use of laboratory-based technologies to alter or change an organism's genetic makeup (NHGRI, 2026) This emerging technology deals more with the biological side of things.

With the continuous development of gene-related technologies, we are able to battle diseases and illnesses that humans spent hundreds if not thousands of years fighing. Similar to other emerging technologies, Genetic Engineering also shows great promise and potential to be a massive help to humanity.

But then again, there exists disadvantages for most emerging technologies. Similarly to Advanced Robotics- Genetic Engineering could allow other individuals to create something undesireable. Even with the risks and benefits, just like most forms and types of emerging technology, Genetic Engineering has so much potential and opportunities.
