Introduction to Emerging Technologies

Emerging technologies are innovative solutions that are currently being developed or implemented to address existing challenges and create new opportunities.
These technologies often have the potential to significantly impact various industries and aspects of society.

Emerging technologies are typically new technologies discovered through human ingenuity
But! they do include old technologies that serve new purposes.

Some examples of Emerging Technologies include

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Nanotechnology
  • Virtual Reality
  • Robotics
  • Quantum Computing
  • Biotechnology

These technologies are constantly evolving and have the potential to transform the way we live, work, and interact with the world around us.
