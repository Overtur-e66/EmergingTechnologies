Introduction to Emerging Technologies

Emerging technologies are innovative solutions that are currently being developed or implemented to address existing challenges and create new opportunities. These technologies often have the potential to significantly impact various industries and aspects of society.

Emerging technologies are typically new technologies discovered through human ingenuity, But! they do include old technologies that serve new purposes.

Some examples of Emerging Technologies include

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Nanotechnology
  • Virtual Reality
  • Robotics
  • Quantum Computing
  • Biotechnology

These technologies are constantly evolving and have the potential to transform the way we live, work, and interact with the world around us.

Ajayi et. al., 2025

Emerging technologies are characterized by radical novelty (in application even if not in origins) relatively fast growth, coherence, prominent impact, and uncertainty and ambiguity.

Rotolo et. al., 2015

Types of Emerging Technologies: Artificial Intelligence

According to NASA (2024) Artificial Intelligence refers to computer systems that are capable of performing complex tasks normally done by people.
